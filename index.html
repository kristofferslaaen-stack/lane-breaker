Lanebreaker

Buy an army, arrange its formation, and let it march every 35 seconds. Break the enemy citadel.

How it works. Units you buy stay in your camp. Every 35 s a copy of your camp marches in the same formation and fights for up to 130 s. Drag units in the camp to rearrange them. In 3 vs 3 your team takes turns: one player per team marches each wave. You earn 100 gold per 20 s; each Gold Extractor adds 10 and holding the middle counts as one more. Tier 2 and Tier 3 unlock stronger units. Summon up to three heroes in the Heroes tab; they level up from kills near them, learn skills you choose, and carry up to six items from the Merchant in the Items tab. In Mixed and Draft you also earn 1 crystal per second for mercenaries.
